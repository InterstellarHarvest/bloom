Four variations inside the narrowed direction from the owner's review of Round 1. They share one design language and differ on purpose in a few places: where region details appear, where Adapt / Spread / Terraform open, how the Regions browser sits beside the map and how the heatmap lens is tucked away. All four keep a large map, a live HUD, and persistent Pause and speed controls.

What the owner asked for (from the Round 1 review)

✓ Kept

  • Map-first play with a large, visually dominant map
  • Concept 2's Regions browser and its triage information
  • A rich region popup with tabs. No “More ▾”.
  • A medium specimen where it adds information: the region popup and Adapt
  • Small floating Adapt / Spread / Terraform launchers (Concept 4 scale)
  • Heatmaps (Temperature, Water, Soil, Hazard) behind one compact control
  • Persistent Pause / Play and speed (1× → 2× → 4×)

✕ Rejected

  • A permanent giant specimen (Concept 3)
  • Full-screen Adapt / Spread / Terraform takeovers (Concept 1)
  • Giant permanent bottom action buttons (Concept 1)

  • So little information that it feels like a watching game (Concept 4)
  • Four always-visible heatmap buttons (Concept 2)

  1. Concept 5: the baseline answer to the review. Click 4–5 regions, use every popover tab, open each action palette, try Pause and speed.
  2. Concept 7: same inspection, but changes happen in a bigger bottom workbench. Is the extra card room worth covering the lower map?

Questions to keep in mind

  • Can I always see something changing, and something I could decide?
  • Is Pause / speed easy to find without looking for it?
  • Is the popover big enough without “More”? Too big?
  • Anchored next to the region, or docked at the edge?
  • Palette, card stack, bottom workbench or side workbench for upgrades?
  • Which lens treatment is easiest to find and to turn off?

Four clickable layout ideas for the run screen, so the owner and PMO can compare where things live and how it feels before the real UI and specimen work is authorized. All four use the same fake planet and the same fake numbers, so any difference you notice is the layout.

★ PMO-RECOMMENDED BASELINE

Concept 1Hybrid: map + region drawer + big boards

BIOMASS · GOAL · SCENARIO MAP REGION DRAWER
+ specimen ADAPT SPREAD TERRAFORM

The map stays the centre. Tapping a region slides in a context drawer showing your plant in that region, what is holding it back, and tabs for conditions, colony growth and raw details. Adapt, Spread and Terraform are big, dedicated boards launched from a chunky bottom bar. Each board takes over the play area while Biomass stays visible, and puts a large specimen next to the cards.

Try this

  • Click Frost Ridge, then press ❄️ Cold Tolerance inside the drawer. It opens Adapt with that card focused.
  • On a board, hover cards: the plant changes and the inset map marks + opens and − harder. Buy one.
  • Open Terraform and hover Humidify: the sky and soil change, the plant doesn't.
  • Select Mossy Basin → Colony tab. Hover Roots, Leaves and Seeds and watch the specimen.
  • Press Esc to close boards and the drawer. Collect a gold + bubble on the map.

🌿 Specimen: in the region drawer (contextual), and large on every board.

Concept 2Drawer-heavy map-first

BIOMASS · ADVISOR · SCENARIO ⋮ SHEET
cards MAP LENSES TABBED
REGION DRAWER

A SimCity / Sims style information layout (their organisation, not their look). Nothing is modal: a tool rail opens side sheets that share the screen with a rich, tabbed region drawer, and the map stays clickable the whole time. Hovering a card previews its effect on the real map. Map lenses recolour the planet by one condition, and an advisor line narrates.

Try this

  • Open Adapt from the rail and hover cards: the main map shows + and − directly.
  • Switch the map lens to Temp or Water.
  • Open Regions and sort “needs help first”. Click one.
  • Use the drawer's vertical tabs: Colony (big focus cards) and Nearby (jump to neighbours).
  • Notice how small the map gets with both panels open at laptop width.

🌿 Specimen: a small round portrait in the drawer header, plus a small preview in the sheet.

Concept 3Specimen companion

BIOMASS · GOAL · SCENARIO YOUR PLANT
(always on) ADAPT SPREAD MAP TERRAFORM

The plant is a permanent companion beside the map. Controls are split by what they change: Adapt, Spread and colony growth sit under the plant, Terraform sits on the map. Selecting a region shows your plant living there, so clicking around the map doubles as “how would my plant do here?”. Adapt panels slide up beneath the plant. Terraform slides up beneath the map and changes both the map tint and the sky around the plant.

Try this

  • Click different regions and watch the plant's world change (snow, haze, salt, ash).
  • Hover the adaptation tags under the plant: the matching body part lights up.
  • Hover the SKY & SOIL and PLANT labels on the scene.
  • Open Adapt and hover Drought Adaptation. Then open Terraform and hover Dry the Sky. Compare.

🌿 Specimen: permanent and large, follows the selected region.

FULL-SCREEN MAP ✦ 264 ◔ ◔ CARD 🌿 🌱 | 🌍

A Terra Nil-like calm (its restraint, not its look). The planet fills the screen and almost nothing is permanent. Biomass floats in one corner and the goal and pressure rings in the other. A small card pops up beside the region you tap. A compact shelf at the bottom expands into one row of cards. One thing at a time, details only on request (“More ▾”).

Try this

  • Click Marsh Low: use the round focus icons, then ＋ Upgrade and More ▾.
  • Open Spread on the shelf and hover Waterborne Seeds: a seed path appears across the sea.
  • Click the BLOOM and AIR rings.

🌿 Specimen: a small portrait in the region card and in the shelf tray. Never a panel.

Suggested review order

  1. Concept 4 first, for about 2 minutes, to calibrate how little interface the game could get away with.
  2. Concept 1, the recommended baseline. Spend the most time here.
  3. Concept 3: does an always-visible plant earn its screen space?
  4. Concept 2: is non-modal, everything-at-once better or just busier?
  5. Then pick and mix: e.g. “Concept 1, but with Concept 2's lenses and Concept 3's plant/planet split”.

Questions to keep in mind

  • What do I look at first?
  • Does the map stay central?
  • Where does Biomass live? Is it always visible?
  • Where does scenario pressure live?
  • Where do region details live?
  • Where should colony growth controls live?
  • How much specimen: always on, or only in context?
  • Should Adapt / Spread / Terraform be big boards, side sheets or a small tray?
  • Is “this changes my plant” vs “this changes the planet” obvious?
  • Does it feel like a desktop game, an iPad app, or both?

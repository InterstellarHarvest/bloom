# BLOOM — Frozen World playtest v1 (owner)

**What this tests:** whether the third archetype, **Frozen World**, feels like a genuinely different planet from Ocean Archipelago and Desert World. Here the problem is **temperature**. The validator has already shown that these worlds can be won (layers 1–8) in at least two broad ways: evolving for the cold, or warming the sky. Only a person can say whether the cold reads clearly and whether that choice feels real.

**Gate status:** ☑ **passed** (owner, recorded in BLOOM-012): *"all good"*.

**Time needed:** one run of roughly 10–20 minutes, plus a few minutes for the questions. The second run is optional.

---

## Before you play

- **Don't look up solutions.** Don't read `tools/frozen-check.js`, `docs/evidence/bloom-011/` or the README's QA section first: they name the winning builds for these exact seeds.
- Play at **1×** with the normal controls only (map, shop, colony focus buttons, bubbles, pause). Judge the decisions, not the art.
- New readouts to look at: a region's panel now shows its **ground temperature in °C** and the range that suits your plant. Hovering **Warm the Sky** lists every region the warmer sky would open, move closer, or make worse. After a Terraform purchase, the HUD shows the change (▲ +6°C) and the map outlines the regions affected for a few seconds.

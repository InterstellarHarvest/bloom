# BLOOM — Native Competition playtest v1 (owner)

**What's new:** this planet is already alive. Native vegetation grows on part of the land when you arrive, spreads into open ground, and competes with your plant for light, water, nutrients and space. There is no timer. You still win by holding **70%** of the land with living plants at once. You never have to remove every native plant.

**Time needed:** one run, about 5–12 minutes, plus nine short answers.

**Gate status:** ☐ not yet played.

## How to read it

- **Violet, hatched patches** are native plants. Dense stands are darker and carry two marks per tile.
- **Amber lines** mark the fronts: places where your plants touch native plants.
- **Click a region** to see its 🌿 **Competition** line: who has the advantage there, and why.
- The **bar under the Biomass counter** shows how much land the native plants hold, whether they are spreading or retreating, and how many regions are contested.
- Where the two plants meet, the one **better suited to that ground** pushes into the other. When both suit the ground equally, **established stands hold** and young ones can be overgrown.
- Your usual tools all apply. **Adapt** makes your plant fit better. **Terraform** changes the sky for both plants, so it can weaken native strongholds, but it may help them elsewhere; the purchase preview says where. **Roots / Seeds / Leaves** focus works per colony as before.
- If your plants die out everywhere for 8 seconds, the run is lost (extinction).

## Launch

Open `demos/demo-run.html`, add this to the end of the address, and press Enter:

`?archetype=desert_world&seed=25&scenario=native_competition`

Play at **1×** with the normal controls. Click bubbles or ignore them, whichever you'd normally do.

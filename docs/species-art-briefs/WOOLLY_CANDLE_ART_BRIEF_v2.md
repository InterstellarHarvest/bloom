## 1. The species

- **Role:** cold specialist (+ radiation, latent) (concept C1). Species id `woolly_candle` → `{ bodyPlan: "candle@2", pack: "woolly-candle" }` once accepted.
- **Silhouette:** a stout, upright woolly trunk wearing a skirt of dead leaves, topped by a tight rosette of silver-haired leaves; a tall candle-like flower column when it flowers — tall, vertical and soft-edged, the opposite of the dry rosette.
- **Anatomy:** an authored TRUNK sprite (wool + marcescent skirt, one drawing per Cold architecture) + a radial CROWN rosette on the trunk top + a procedural inflorescence COLUMN for reproduction; a taproot.
- **Inspiration:** Andean frailejón (Espeletia), East-African giant groundsels and lobelias (inspiration only — never copied).
- **Innate strength in the base body:** A cold specialist is ALREADY insulated at tier 0: `body.trunk.0` already carries wool and a light skirt; its leaves are already silver-haired and it carries a dark-red pigment tint in its BASE palette (radiation is a latent strength). Buying Cold thickens the skirt and folds the crown; buying Heat is drawn too (lifted, waxed leaves).
- **Room part labels** (the rooms keep the seven logical parts; this plan renames): `stem` → "Trunk".

## 8. Palette, pixels, mirrors

- **18 materials** in the shared contract order (`art/plant/contract.json`): `ink` (1), `leaf` (4), `stem` (4), `root` (4), `core` (4), `petal` (4), `center` (4), `pappus` (4), `seed` (4), `frost` (4), `tuber` (4), `salt` (4), `pod` (4), `pigLeaf`* (4), `pigStem`* (4), `pigCore`* (4), `waxLeaf`* (4), `waxCore`* (4) — * = treatment target only, NEVER drawn. Shade 0 → 3 runs dark → light (treatments map shade for shade; Heat T1 wax touches shades 2–3). Every colour unique across the pack, `#rrggbb` lower-case; required: ink, leaf, stem, root, core, frost, salt, pod, pigLeaf, pigStem, pigCore, waxLeaf, waxCore.
- **Environment** (specimen box, separate from the plant palette): sky [top, bottom], soil [stratum A, stratum B, pebble], turf; optional `render.stemOutline` self / ink / none for the procedural parts.
- **Alpha:** every pixel is fully transparent (alpha 0) or fully opaque (alpha 255); partial alpha is a build error
- **Palette:** every opaque pixel inside a sprite rect must be EXACTLY one of the pack's declared palette colours (atlas.json palette); arbitrary RGBA is a build error. A colour means a material + shade, so the renderer can recolour it (treatments, condition).
- **Strays / masks:** no opaque pixel may lie outside every declared rect (sprite, mask, swatch); a mask rect has the sprite's exact size; any opaque pixel (any colour) = inside the mask, transparent = outside
- **Mirrors:** right-facing components are drawn facing right; the build bakes the left twin (anchor x and points mirrored) unless an explicit `orientation: "left"` drawing of the same id exists. No rotation ever happens at runtime; every angle is a drawing.

# BLOOM — Volatile Climate playtest v1 (owner)

**What's new:** Terraforming now has a side effect. Each Terraform step unsettles the part of the climate it changes (temperature or moisture). Several steps close together pile up; the climate settles back on its own. When one part of the climate gets unsettled enough, it swings for a while: a **heat pulse, cold snap, wet surge or dry spell**. You get a warning a few seconds ahead, the swing lasts under a minute, and your Terraform stays when it passes. Adapting your plant adds no instability. There is no timer. You still win by holding **70%** of the land with living plants at once.

**Time needed:** one run, about 4–8 minutes, plus nine short answers.

**Gate status:** ☐ not yet played.

## How to read it

- The **bar under the Biomass counter** shows climate instability. It gives the overall % and its state (Stable / Unsettled / Volatile / Critical), the % for temperature and for moisture, any coming or running shock (with its time), a one-line forecast, and the sky written as *base + Terraform + shock*.
- **Hover a Terraform button** before buying. The message says which regions it opens or closes, how much it raises instability, and whether it will set off a shock (and which kind).
- During a shock the map takes a faint tint. Regions it pushes toward their limit (or that reopen as it passes) get a short outline and a message.
- **Click a region** during a shock to see the swing in its temperature line.
- If your plants die out everywhere for 8 seconds, the run is lost (extinction). High instability alone never loses.

## Launch

Open `demos/demo-run.html`, add this to the end of the address, and press Enter:

`?archetype=frozen_world&seed=22&scenario=volatile_climate`

This is the Frozen World you played before. It has two strong answers to the cold: evolve deeper **Cold Tolerance**, or **Warm the Sky**. Play at **1×** with the normal controls, and play the way you'd like to; there's no need to test both.
